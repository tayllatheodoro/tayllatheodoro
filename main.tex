% === EXPERIENCE ===
        \section{\mysidestyle Research and Work Experience}
        \textbf{\href{https://biohub.org/comp-imaging/people/}{Computational Imaging - Chan Zuckerberg Biohub SF}} \hfill \textsl{Aug 2024 -- present}  \\
        \textit{R\&D Engineer} \quad Microscope automation and high-throughput analysis of live-cell imaging.
        \begin{list2}
            \item Built DynaTrack, which lets the microscope follow developing zebrafish neuromasts on its own. It reconstructs phase and virtually stains nuclei during acquisition, so tracking needs only gentle bright-field light. It kept 73 of 75 neuromasts in view for up to 70.5\,h; before, they drifted out of view after about 12\,h~\cite{theodoro2026dynatrack}.
            \item Curated the live-cell dataset shared by DynaCLR and DynaCell: registered the volumes, segmented nuclei from virtual stains, tracked cells with Ultrack, and organized everything as OME-Zarr. Thousands of infected and drug-treated cells over 3D time-lapses. DynaCLR's infection classifier (F1 98.4) was trained on it~\cite{pradeep2024contrastivelearningcellstate,hirata2025mapping,hirata-miyasaki2025embedding}.
            \item Curated and registered the DynaCell benchmark for dynamic 3D virtual staining: paired label-free and fluorescence volumes of A549 cells, four organelles, three infection conditions, imaged on the Mantis microscope~\cite{kalinin2026dynacell}.
        \end{list2}
        \vspace{-0.2cm}

        \textbf{\href{https://lids.ic.unicamp.br/people}{LIDS - Laboratory of Image Data Science, UNICAMP}} \hfill \textsl{Aug 2021 -- 2025}  \\
        \textit{Graduate Researcher} \quad Advisor: Prof.\ Alexandre Xavier Falc\~ao
        \begin{list2}
            \item Worked on finding Early Contrast Enhancement, an MRI sign of pleural mesothelioma, automatically in 4D (3D+t) DCE-MRI of the thorax.
            \item Built Meso-Regions, an open-source pipeline that flags suspicious pleural regions with no manual ROI drawing: ANTs motion correction, pleural extraction with the image foresting transform, SLIC/DISF supervoxels, enhancement curves per supervoxel, and a rule-based detector. Presented at IEEE ISBI 2026~\cite{11515662,theodoro2025mesothelioma}.
            \item Trained a PyTorch U-Net, with diffusion-based augmentation, to segment the pleural region that contains the tumor. Co-authored a superpixel tutorial at SIBGRAPI and the iMig imaging review~\cite{9643120,ARMATO2024107832}.
        \end{list2}
